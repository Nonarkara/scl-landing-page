# SCL Design Contract

Register: Civic programme brief for Thai public and private leaders, usually opened from LINE on a phone.

Design read: A compact depa programme archive with a full-width cohort photograph, confident navy type on white, and yellow guiding the next action.

Reference: MoMA's public website, particularly its direct navigation, image-led composition and tightly aligned content. Apply the project's MoMA rules: invisible grid, compact spacing, distinct structural and cell line weights, simple mechanics, consistent alignment.

Palette: Preserve the original depa yellow (#FFF200), dark blue (#0C2F53), and white. This user-requested brand palette overrides the workspace's default amber. White carries reading; navy carries text and bounded structural fields; yellow marks actions and selection. Secondary text uses contrast-tested neutral blue-grey. No additional decorative colours.

Type: IBM Plex Sans Thai preserves the existing non-looped Thai identity. Label, body and title sizes are shared across routes. The programme name alone uses a larger display size, a deliberate first-viewport brand signal. Tracking is zero. Font sizes change at fixed breakpoints, never with viewport-width formulas.

Density: 7/10. Design variance: 4/10. Motion: 2/10. Use compact section spacing without shrinking controls below 44px. Sections are unframed; images, rows and text share grid edges. Structural rules are 3px; group rules are 2px; row separators are 1px. No decorative gradients, texture, shadows or rounded rectangles. A dark overlay on the hero photograph is for text contrast.

Conservation: All six confirmed cohort rosters, photos, research, interactive maps, charts, filters and routes remain available. Total alumni records remain 306, including 50 in SCL #6. Design work must not change roster data.

Verification: Check all routes in EN, TH and CN at desktop and 320/390px widths, both themes, keyboard-only FAQ, navigation, dialogs, filters and gallery. Automated accessibility checks supplement manual visual review. Commit, push, deploy through GitHub Actions, and check the deployed bundle and browser flows.
